Field Operations & Engineering Standards

Hard Elements Only: Stone, Brick, Block & Earthwork

Harrison Hardscaping LLC specializes strictly in permanent structural hardscaping and raw dirt/grade engineering. By focusing 100% of our equipment, crew training, and physical labor on masonry, stone, aggregate, and earthmoving—rather than seasonal plant maintenance—we deliver construction-grade durability on every residential and commercial site.

The Zero-Shortcut Field Mandate

Every wall, patio, walkway, staircase, and drainage trench is excavated below frost-heave zones, stabilized with non-woven geotextile fabric where soil conditions demand, and built atop mechanically compacted crushed aggregate.

Technical Scope of Work

Structural Masonry & Earthwork Specifications

Scope 01 // Load-Bearing Walls

Retaining Walls & Structural Grade Retention

Engineered to resist lateral earth pressure, clay expansion, and hillside water migration. We construct both dry-stacked segmental walls and mortared natural stone walls.

Construction & Material Standards

  • Excavation & Leveling Pad: Entrenched below grade with 6" to 12"+ of vibratory-compacted crushed stone leveling base.
  • Drainage Core: Continuous 12"+ wide column of washed angular #57 stone directly behind the wall face wrapped in filtration fabric.
  • Hydrostatic Relief: 4" perforated rigid or corrugated drain pipe pitched to daylight weep outlets or storm tie-ins.
  • Soil Reinforcement: Biaxial geogrid tie-back layers integrated into compacted backfill lifts for tall or load-bearing walls.
  • Finish Cap Units: Heavy stone or architectural concrete caps secured with high-strength masonry adhesive or mortar beds.

Scope 02 // Horizontal Stone & Brick

Patios, Walkways, Driveways & Aprons

Heavy-duty horizontal stone, brick, and paver installations engineered for zero pooling, crisp geometric borders, and decades of freeze-thaw resilience.

Construction & Material Standards

  • Sub-Base Depth: 6" to 8" compacted stone base for pedestrian patios/walkways; 10" to 12"+ for vehicular driveways and aprons.
  • Open-Graded & Traditional Bases: Built using either dense-graded CR-6 with concrete sand screed or modern open-graded #57/#89 stone bases.
  • Natural Stone Options: Full-color and blue-select Pennsylvania Bluestone (wet-laid on concrete slab or dry-laid over aggregate), granite cobbles, and travertine.
  • Brick & Paver Options: Clay street pavers, architectural concrete pavers, soldiers-course borders, and herringbone/running-bond layouts.
  • Joint Stabilization: Commercial-grade polymeric locking sand or hand-tooled Type N/S mortar joints.

Scope 03 // Earthmoving & Hydrology

Dirt Grading, Excavation & Stormwater Control

Before stone can stand, the earth beneath and around it must be mastered. We solve chronic yard flooding, foundation water intrusion, and uneven terrain through raw dirt work.

Construction & Material Standards

  • Foundation Re-Grading: Reshaping dirt contours around residential and commercial structures to establish positive pitch away from basement walls.
  • Sub-Surface French Drains: Hand- and machine-dug trenches lined with non-woven geotextile, perforated pipe, and washed river/crushed stone.
  • Downspout & Surface Catch Basins: Solid Schedule 40 PVC or heavy HDPE underground discharge lines routed to pop-up emitters or dry wells.
  • Gravel Pads & Driveways: Excavation, soil stabilization, and compacted crushed-stone pads for sheds, heavy equipment, and country lanes.
  • Spoil Haul-Out & Clean Fill: Removal of excess clay, rock, concrete rubble, and delivery of compactable structural dirt fill.
Dirt Re-Grading Schedule 40 Drainage Gravel Pads
Scope 04 // Mortared Stone & Brick

Steps, Seat Walls, Columns & Masonry Repair

Classical trowel-and-hammer masonry for vertical architectural features, grade transitions, and structural restoration of aging brickwork and stonework.

Construction & Material Standards

  • Stone & Brick Steps: Poured concrete or compacted stone foundations topped with solid 6" stone treads, bluestone risers, or mortared brick treads.
  • Freestanding Seat Walls & Pillars: 18" to 24" sitting walls and monumental entry piers built with CMU block cores and natural stone or brick veneer.
  • Outdoor Fire Features: Custom stone fire pits, seat-wall enclosures, and fire-brick-lined masonry hearths.
  • Tuckpointing & Restoration: Grinding out deteriorated mortar joints, resetting loose stone or brick, and matching mortar strength and color.

Field Execution Protocol

Six Phases of a Harrison Hardscaping Build

Every project follows a structured engineering sequence from initial grade assessment to final joint lock.

PHASE 01 // SURVEY

Grade, Soil & Drainage Audit

We evaluate slope elevations, water runoff paths, soil composition, and underground utility clearances before breaking ground.

PHASE 02 // EXCAVATION

Demolition & Dirt Stripping

Failed concrete or old pavers are demolished and hauled off-site, and unstable topsoil is excavated down to solid virgin sub-grade.

PHASE 03 // HYDROLOGY

Drainage & Geotextile Armor

Underground drain pipes, weep channels, and soil-separation geotextiles are installed so water never undermines the structure.

PHASE 04 // SUB-BASE

Compacted Stone Lifts

Crushed stone aggregate is placed and mechanically compacted in measured lifts to achieve maximum load-bearing density.

PHASE 05 // MASONRY

Precision Setting & Cutting

Stone, brick, and block are set to string-line and laser tolerances, with wet-saw diamond cuts for tight architectural corners.

PHASE 06 // LOCKDOWN

Jointing, Edge Restraint & Grade

Edges are locked in steel or concrete, joints are sealed with polymeric sand or mortar, and surrounding dirt grades are dressed clean.
