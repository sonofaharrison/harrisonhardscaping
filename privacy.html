Privacy Policy & Digital Asset Governance

Harrison Hardscaping LLC (harrisonhardscaping.com), operating under the Sons of Harrison corporate nexus (sonsofharrison.com), enforces strict client confidentiality, zero third-party ad-tracker bloat, and uncompromising intellectual property protection.

Section 01 // Zero Ad-Tech Surveillance

Client & Property Confidentiality

We do not sell, broker, or syndicate client contact records, property addresses, site survey photographs, or structural grading plans to third-party data brokers or marketing networks. Information shared with Harrison Hardscaping LLC is used strictly to evaluate, engineer, estimate, and execute your masonry or earthwork project.

Section 02 // Client-Side Interface State

Transparent localStorage Architecture

This web property operates without invasive tracking cookies. To preserve your visual display preferences across pages, your browser stores three lightweight client-side keys in localStorage:

  • harrison-theme: Stores your selected site theme (dark Obsidian Basalt or light Alabaster Marble).
  • harrison-hardscaping-crest: Stores your selected 9:16 Hero emblem stage finish.
  • harrison-view-mode: Stores your layout view state (standard responsive desktop or mobile center-scroll simulator).
