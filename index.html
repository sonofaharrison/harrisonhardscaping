Core Field Capabilities

Four Divisions of Heavy Physical Craftsmanship

Every project is executed through disciplined manual labor, laser-verified grading, and over-engineered stone foundations. Zero softscaping—strictly structural earth and stone.

Division I // Earth Retention

Engineered Retaining Walls & Slope Terracing

Holding back tons of saturated Maryland soil requires more than stacked block—it requires deep trenching, compacted aggregate footings, geogrid soil reinforcement, and hydrostatic relief.

  • Segmental Retaining Wall (SRW) systems and heavy architectural block
  • Natural quarry stone, dry-stack fieldstone, and mortared stone walls
  • Hillside cut-and-fill terracing to reclaim steep, unusable yard grades
  • Washed #57 stone backfill columns with perforated drain tile outlets
Geogrid Tie-Backs Hydrostatic Drainage Grade Stabilization
Division II // Architectural Flatwork

Stone Patios, Interlocking Pavers & Brick Walkways

Precision-laid outdoor living surfaces, walkways, and heavy-load driveways built over deep, mechanically compacted crushed-stone sub-bases that resist frost heave and settling.

  • Thermal and natural cleft Pennsylvania Bluestone patios and landings
  • High-density interlocking concrete pavers and permeable paver systems
  • Traditional mortared and sand-set clay brick walkways, borders, and aprons
  • Reinforced steel-stake or concrete-curb edge restraints with polymeric joint locking
Bluestone & Granite Interlocking Pavers Clay Brick Flatwork
Division III // Dirt, Grading & Drainage

Excavation, Dirt Re-Grading & Sub-Surface Drainage

Water and unstable soil destroy poorly prepped masonry. We reshape raw dirt grades, excavate unstable clay, and install underground drainage networks that protect foundations and hardscapes.

  • Precision dirt grading and positive slope correction away from home foundations
  • Sub-grade excavation, soil haul-out, and compacted structural fill delivery
  • Sub-surface French drains, catch basins, channel drains, and downspout burial
  • Gravel driveway grading, CR-6 crushed stone base installation, and wash-out repair
Laser Pitch Grading French Drains CR-6 & #57 Stone
Division IV // Structural Masonry

Custom Stonework, Brick Columns & Outdoor Hearths

Hand-tooled vertical masonry crafted from natural stone, brick, and concrete block—bringing architectural permanence to entryways, grade transitions, and gathering spaces.

  • Monumental stone and brick entry columns, piers, and perimeter seat walls
  • Solid natural stone slab steps, granite treads, and mortared masonry staircases
  • Custom stone and fire-brick outdoor fireplaces, fire pits, and kitchen enclosures
  • Structural masonry repointing, spalled brick replacement, and stone veneer installation

The Unseen Work Below Grade

Why Our Stonework Never Shifts

Anyone can lay stone on top of dirt and make it look good for a single season. True hardscaping is won in the trench—moving tons of earth and aggregate before the first finish stone is ever set.

PILLAR 01 // EARTHWORK

Deep Sub-Grade Excavation

We strip away all organic topsoil and unstable fill down to virgin sub-soil, establishing laser-verified pitch and geotextile separation so native clay never contaminates your stone base.

PILLAR 02 // COMPACTION

Layered Aggregate Base Lifts

We build foundations in compacted multi-inch lifts of dense-graded crushed aggregate and open-graded stone, locking the sub-base into an unyielding slab that sheds water effortlessly.

PILLAR 03 // CRAFTSMANSHIP

Hand-Chiseled Tight Joints

represented by the silver builder lion holding the spade in our crest, every course of stone, brick, and block is leveled by hand, cut with diamond precision, and locked for decades of service.
